# Course Tool Ideas

These are nascent ideas for tools that support CS Essentials students and teachers. They came from the [Shipyard pep rally brainstorm](https://github.com/riverside-pirates/shipyard/blob/main/ideas/2026-10-11-pep-rally-brainstorm.md) on October 11, 2026. None of them are bounties yet.

## Due Date Dock, `hard`
- **Pitched by:** Jay (9th grader). Built on by Mr. B.
- **Problem:** As a student, I need to know what's due this week, but due dates are scattered across classes and tools.
- **Twist from Mr. B:** Pull dates automatically from course repos and assignments, so teachers don't have to post them twice.
- **Start small:** One page listing upcoming CS Essentials assignments only.

## RubricBot, `legendary`
- **Pitched by:** Mr. B.
- **Problem:** As a teacher, I want to grade the thinking, not the typing, but giving written feedback on every PR takes forever.
- **Idea:** Structured rubric feedback posted on student PRs. This would extend the classroom50 autograding setup.
- **Caution:** Feedback lives on private student repos. Keep it there.

## Offline Lesson Packs, `medium`
- **Pitched by:** Mr. B.
- **Problem:** As a student without home Wi-Fi, I can't do the lesson at home.
- **Idea:** Downloadable or printable packs for each unit that work offline on a Chromebook.
- **Start small:** Turn one lesson into a single self-contained HTML file.
